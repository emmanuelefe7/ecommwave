A customer has one question. Nobody answers it. 👀

"Does it run true to size?" If your product page doesn't say, and the only way to ask is a contact form, the customer waits… and buys from a store that answered.

✅ Put the top questions customers ask right under the price (a short FAQ)
✅ Add a chat that shows how fast you usually reply
✅ Keep ready replies for the questions you get every day, so you answer in seconds

Answer before they leave, and more of those questions turn into orders.
