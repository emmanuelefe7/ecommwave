Last-minute shoppers can't decide what to buy? Sell them a gift card.

Gift cards are the easy answer for "I don't know what they like" and for orders that are too late to ship. Swipe for 4 simple fixes 👉

1️⃣ Put gift cards in your main menu and top bar
2️⃣ Offer 3 or 4 clear amounts
3️⃣ Let customers add a note and pick the day it's emailed
4️⃣ After your last shipping day, switch your top bar to gift cards
