Not sure what to fix first in your store? Start here. 👇

Fix things in the order your customer meets them:

1️⃣ Speed on phones: slow on your phone? Fix that first.
2️⃣ Product page: clear photos, reviews, an easy Add to cart.
3️⃣ Checkout: shipping shown early, guest checkout on.
4️⃣ Follow-up emails: bring back customers who left their cart.
