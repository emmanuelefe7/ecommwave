Selling to customers in other countries? Show them prices in their own money.

A price in a currency they don't use makes customers stop and do math. Many just leave.

🌍 Turn on local currencies, so prices show in euros, pounds or dollars automatically
🚚 Show how long shipping takes to their country, right on the product page
🛒 Keep the same currency from product page to checkout, no surprises

📌 Save this for when you start selling abroad.
