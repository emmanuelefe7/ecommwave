Customers want to buy… but can't find a way to reach you? 📩

If your store has no email, no chat and no real name anywhere, a new customer starts to wonder if anyone is behind it. And when they're not sure, they don't buy.

✅ Make a clear Contact page (link it in the menu and footer)
✅ Show an email address and a chat
✅ Say how fast you usually reply
