"It looked bigger in the photo." Heard that from a customer? 👜

A beautiful close-up can make a small product look huge. Then the package arrives, and so does the return request.

✅ Put the size right on the photo: width, height, depth
✅ Add one photo of the product in someone's hand, so the scale is clear
✅ Say what fits inside (or what it fits with)

Fewer returns 02 of 3.
