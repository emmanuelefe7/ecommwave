Customers buying gifts early? Tell them they can still return it in January.

Many holiday customers hold back because they're worried the gift won't fit or won't be loved. A longer return window takes that worry away.

🗓️ Extend returns for holiday orders (for example, until January 31)
🛒 Say it right under Add to cart, and in your top bar
🧾 Offer a gift receipt with no price on it
🔁 Make swaps for another size or color quick and easy
