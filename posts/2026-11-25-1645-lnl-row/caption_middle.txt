"Will it arrive before Christmas?" If your store doesn't answer that, customers buy somewhere that does. 🎁

Holiday customers care about one thing more than price: getting it on time. Make the answer impossible to miss:

1️⃣ Put your last order date in the top bar of your store
2️⃣ Show "arrives before Dec 24" next to Add to cart
3️⃣ Remind them of the cutoff in the cart
4️⃣ After your last ship date, switch to digital gift cards

Use your carrier's real cutoff dates. The dates in these slides are just an example.
