Customers buy once… then never come back? 👀

Getting the first order is the hard part. The second one should be easier. Follow the mint path:

1️⃣ Say thank you right away — a clear thank-you email with what happens next.
2️⃣ Tell them when it ships — a tracking update so they're never left guessing.
3️⃣ Ask for a review after delivery — once they've actually used it.
4️⃣ Give them a reason to come back — new picks that go with what they bought.

That's how first-time customers turn into repeat customers. 🔁
