Your shoppers can't touch your product. Your photos have to do that job. 📸

Online, the photo is the product. Before you spend on ads, make sure your photos answer the questions a shopper would ask in a real store:

✅ Clean background: nothing distracts from the product
✅ Show it in use: help them picture owning it
✅ Every angle: front, back, side and inside
✅ Close-up details: texture, size and quality
