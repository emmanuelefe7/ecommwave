Too many returns? Check your size guide first. 📏

When customers have to guess their size, many order two and send one back. Or order the wrong one and return it.

✅ Put a "Size guide" link right next to the size buttons, not in the footer
✅ Give real measurements: bust, waist, length, in cm and inches
✅ Say what the model wears ("175 cm, wears size S")
