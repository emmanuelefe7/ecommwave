New customers check your About page before they buy. What do they find? 👀

When people don't know your store yet, they look for proof that real people are behind it. A good About page gives them that:

👋 Why you started: a real reason, in a few short lines
🙂 Who's behind it: real photos of you and your team
⭐ What makes you different: three clear reasons to pick you
🛍️ A next step: a few reviews and a button back to your best sellers
