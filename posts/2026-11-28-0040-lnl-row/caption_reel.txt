A customer loves your product… but not today. Do they ever come back?

Most stores let "maybe later" customers walk away for good. Give them a way to save it:

❤️ Add a heart button to every product, so one tap saves it for later
📩 Send a friendly reminder email about the items they saved
👗 Tell them when their size is back in stock
🛒 Bring them back ready to buy, from the same visitors you already have
