Your sale timer says "10 minutes left"… every single day? 👀

Customers notice. They come back tomorrow, see the same timer, and stop trusting it. Then they stop trusting your prices too.

✅ Only use a countdown when the deadline is real
✅ Show the real end date ("Sale ends Sunday at midnight")
✅ When the sale ends, the price goes back. Every time.

Honest deadlines build trust, and trust is what turns visitors into customers.
