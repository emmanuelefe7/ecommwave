=== _1 (top-left) ===
New visitors don’t trust your store yet? Show your reviews right by the price.

Building customer trust starts on the product page. A first-time visitor doesn’t know you, so they look for proof that other people bought and were happy.

⭐ Put the star rating and review count right under the price, not at the bottom of the page
💬 Show one short, real review near the Add to cart button
✅ Mark reviews from verified customers
🙋 No reviews yet? Ask your first customers after delivery

=== _2 (top-middle) ===
No contact info on your store? Strangers won’t pay you.

Before someone orders from a new Shopify store, they want to know there are real people behind it, people they can reach if something goes wrong.

📧 Show a real email address on a clear Contact page
💬 Add a chat and say how fast you usually reply
📞 A phone number and opening hours help too
📍 Say where you’re based and where orders ship from

=== _3 (top-right) ===
Returns policy hidden in your footer? Put it next to Add to cart.

Customers worry “what if it doesn’t fit?” right when they’re about to add to cart. That’s where the answer should be, not three clicks away.

🚚 Shipping cost or free-shipping amount
📅 When it will arrive
↩️ Your returns promise in one short line
Keep all three right under the Add to cart button. Short, clear, easy to spot.

=== _4 (bottom-left) ===
Customers stop at checkout? Show them it’s safe to pay.

On a store they’ve never bought from, customers hesitate before typing their card details. Small trust signals at checkout make that moment easier.

🔒 Say “Secure checkout” at the top of checkout
💳 Offer the payment options your customers already know and use
✅ Put a short “encrypted, secure payment” line right by the Pay button
🧾 Show the full total, shipping included, before they pay

Faceless store with no story? Show customers who’s behind it.

People trust people. A real About page is one of the easiest ways to build customer trust on a new Shopify store.

👋 A real photo of you or your team
✍️ Two or three lines on why you started and what you care about
