Making customers sign up before they can pay? That's a sale walking out the door. 🚪

Picture it: they love the product, tap Add to cart, head to checkout… and get "Create an account to continue." Email, password, confirm password. They just wanted to buy a dress. So they close the tab.

The fix:
✅ Let customers check out as a guest
✅ Ask only for what you need to ship the order
✅ Offer the account AFTER the order, with one tap ("save my details for next time")
