Only photos on your product page? Add a short video.

Photos show what it looks like. A video shows how it moves, fits and feels.

🎥 Keep it short, about 10 to 15 seconds
👗 Show the product in use: worn, opened, poured, carried
🔇 Make it work without sound, most people watch muted
📍 Put it second in the gallery, right after the main photo
