Most product descriptions just list features. The ones that sell help the shopper picture the result:

✅ Lead with the benefit, not just what it is
✅ Use short bullet points that are easy to scan on a phone
✅ Answer real questions: size, materials, care, delivery
✅ Write like you talk: friendly and simple, no hype
