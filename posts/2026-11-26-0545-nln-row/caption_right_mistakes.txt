Can customers even spot your Add to cart button? 👀

On a lot of stores it blends right in: same color as the page, small, and easy to scroll past. If customers have to look for it, some won't.

✅ Give it one bold color you don't use anywhere else on the page
✅ Make it big enough for a thumb on a phone
✅ Use clear words: "Add to cart" (adding the price helps too)

Open your own product page on your phone. Can you find the button in one second?
