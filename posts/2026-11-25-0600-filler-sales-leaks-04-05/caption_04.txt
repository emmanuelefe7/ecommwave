Customers love it… but can't find the Add to cart button? 📱

On a phone, a long product page pushes the button way down. Every extra scroll is a chance for your customer to give up.

✅ Keep Add to cart stuck to the bottom of the screen on phones
✅ Show the price right next to it
✅ Test it yourself: open your product page on your phone
