Got a bad review? Don't hide from it. Your reply can still win the next sale. ⭐

New customers read the bad reviews AND how you answered them. A calm, helpful reply shows them what happens if something goes wrong.

✅ Reply within a day
✅ Say sorry, then fix it (exchange, refund, a clear next step)
✅ Keep it short and kind, never argue
✅ If the review points to a real problem, fix it on the product page too (like adding fit notes)
