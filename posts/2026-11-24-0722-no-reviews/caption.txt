People visit your product page… but nobody buys? 👀

Check one thing first: does your page have reviews?

With zero reviews, a shopper has no proof your product is any good. So they leave and buy from a store they already trust.

✅ Ask every buyer for a review a few days after delivery
✅ Show the star rating right next to the price
✅ Add photo reviews from real customers
✅ Reply to reviews, good and bad

Reviews do the selling when you can't be there to answer questions.
