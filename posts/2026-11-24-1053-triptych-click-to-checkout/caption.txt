Getting visitors but not enough buyers? 👀

✅ Visit: a clear product page with photos, price, reviews and one big button
✅ Cart: no surprise fees, free-shipping progress, easy to change quantities
✅ Checkout: fast on mobile, no forced account, trusted payment options
Fix the weakest step first, then the next one.
