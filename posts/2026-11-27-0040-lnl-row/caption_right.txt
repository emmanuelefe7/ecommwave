Black Friday is here. Is your Cyber Monday ready? 🗓️

Cyber Monday is November 30. Don't leave it to Monday morning. A simple weekend plan:

1️⃣ Friday to Sunday: keep your abandoned-cart emails on
2️⃣ Sunday: one email to your list with the Monday offer and when it ends
3️⃣ Monday morning: update the top bar and homepage to the new offer
4️⃣ Monday night: show the end time, then end it when you said

Clear dates build trust, and trust brings customers back next year.
