Sold out? Don't let the customer walk away too. 👀

A sold-out page with just a grey button is a dead end. The customer was ready to buy, and now they leave for another store.

✅ Add a "Notify me when it's back" email box
✅ Show 2–3 similar products right under it
✅ Send that email the moment it's back in stock
