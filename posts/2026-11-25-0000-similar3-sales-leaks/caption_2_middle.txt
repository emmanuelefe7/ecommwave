Popups are chasing your customers away. 🚪

A customer opens your product page and gets hit with "10% off!", "Spin to win!" and "Allow notifications?" before they even see the product.

That's a lot of ✕ buttons between them and "Add to cart".

✅ Keep ONE popup
✅ Show it after they've looked around, not the second they land
✅ Make it easy to close on phones
