Customers think your price is too high? 🤔

Often the price isn't the problem. The page just doesn't show what they get for it.

A price on its own gives customers nothing to weigh it against. So put the reasons to say yes right under it:

✅ What it's made of, in plain words
✅ Your return policy (free returns? say it)
✅ How fast it ships

Short lines, right where their eyes already are, before the Add to cart button.
