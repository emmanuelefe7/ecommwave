Does the top bar of your store just say "Welcome to our store!"? 🤔

That thin bar is the first thing every visitor sees, on every page. Make it work for you:

🚚 Your free-shipping amount ("Free shipping over $50")
🎁 Your holiday order-by date ("Order by Dec 18 for Christmas")
↩️ Your returns promise ("Free 30-day returns")

Keep each message short, show one at a time and rotate them. Use your own real amounts and dates.
