Can your customers pay the way they like? 💳

If checkout only offers one way to pay, plus a long card form on a small phone screen, some customers give up right before the order.

✅ Offer the payment options your customers already use
✅ Put the quick, one-tap options at the top of checkout
✅ Show how they can pay before checkout too (near Add to cart)
