Trying to sell everything at once? Here's the thing. 💡

When a new store pushes 50 products at the same time, customers don't know where to look, and your ads and emails get spread thin. Growing stores often start with one hero product.

✅ Put it front and center on your homepage
✅ Use it in your ads and your emails
✅ Collect reviews on it first
✅ Then add more products around it

One clear hero makes your store easier to understand and easier to buy from.
