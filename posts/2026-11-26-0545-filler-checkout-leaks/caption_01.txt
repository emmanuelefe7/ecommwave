Customers reach checkout… then leave to hunt for a discount code? 🏷️

When there's an empty "Discount code" box, many customers stop and wonder if they're missing a deal. They open a new tab to search, and some never come back.

✅ Running a sale? Apply it automatically, so no code is needed
✅ Or show the code right on the page (top bar, product page, cart)
✅ Tell them in the cart that the offer is already applied
