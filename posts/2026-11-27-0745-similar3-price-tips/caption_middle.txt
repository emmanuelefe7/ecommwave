Running a sale? Make the savings obvious. 🏷️

A red "SALE" tag and one price leave customers guessing: sale compared to what?

Show them, on the product page and in the cart:

✅ The old price crossed out next to the new one
✅ What they save ("You save $30")
✅ When the sale really ends

Only do this for real sales, with real old prices. Fake "was" prices hurt trust fast.
