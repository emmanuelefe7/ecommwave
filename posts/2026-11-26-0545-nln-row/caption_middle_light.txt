A customer just ordered from you. What do they see next? 🛍️

Most stores show a plain "Thanks for your order" and that's it. But this is the moment a customer is happiest with you. Use it:

1️⃣ What happens next: when it ships and when it arrives
2️⃣ A reason to come back: a small thank-you offer for the next order
3️⃣ Join the list: first look at new products
4️⃣ Tell a friend: an easy way to share your store

Turn first-time customers into repeat customers, starting on the thank-you page.
