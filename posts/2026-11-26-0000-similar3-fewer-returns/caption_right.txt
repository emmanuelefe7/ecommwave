Getting returns marked "not as described"? Your product page might be the reason. 📦

If customers have to guess the details, they fill the gaps with their own idea. When the real thing is different, it goes back.

✅ Give the exact size or amount ("30 ml", not just "bottle")
✅ Say what it feels like: texture, scent, material
✅ Say who it's for, and who it's not for, so the wrong customer skips it

Fewer returns 03 of 3.
