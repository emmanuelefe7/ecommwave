Big price scaring customers away? Split it. 💳

$129 can feel like a lot to pay at once, even when the customer wants it.

If your checkout already offers pay later (pay in installments), don't hide it until the last step:

✅ Show the split right under the price: "or 4 payments of $32.25"
✅ Show it on every product, on product pages and in collections
✅ Keep the full price clear too, no tricks

Customers who were about to leave now see a number that fits their budget.
