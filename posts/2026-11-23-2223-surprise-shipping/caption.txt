Shoppers add to cart… then vanish at checkout? 🛒

One common reason: a shipping fee they didn't see coming. The total jumps at the last step, trust drops, and the tab gets closed.

✅ Show shipping costs on the product page
✅ Add a clear free shipping goal (like "free over $50")
✅ Show a progress bar in the cart so shoppers can reach it
✅ Keep the checkout total the same as what they expected

No surprises at checkout = a smoother path to "Place order".
