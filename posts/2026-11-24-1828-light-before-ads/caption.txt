Running ads to a store that isn't ready is like paying for visitors who leave. 💸

Before you spend on ads, check these 5 things:

📱 Fast mobile pages: your store loads quickly on any phone
📸 Clear product photos: shoppers can see the product up close
⭐ Reviews near the price: proof right where they decide
🚚 Shipping costs upfront: no surprises at checkout
📩 Abandoned-cart email: win back the almost-buyers

Fix these first, then turn on the ads.
